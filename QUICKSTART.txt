sync -- QUICKSTART
Read this first. The full manual is readme.txt; design rationale and
the changelog are in devnotes.txt.

sync runs a crew of multiboxed FFXI characters from one HUD: healing,
buffing, pulling, weaponskills, skillchains, movement. You play your
main; sync plays the rest.


THE THREE RULES
================================================================
1. ONE BRAIN. sync loads on your MAIN only. Every other box just
   reports in and does what it is told.

2. TWO HELPERS PER BOX. Every box -- main included -- needs
   rdmhelper (reports state) and Multisend (carries commands).
   A box missing either is invisible to sync: it sits there doing
   nothing and its crew row shows a red "! silent" tag.

3. NOTHING RUNS UNASSIGNED. A box does not heal because it is a
   WHM. It heals because you put it in a healer slot. Same for
   RDM, BRD, COR, GEO. Job alone does nothing.

The whole loop: assign roles -> /sync on -> play your main.


INSTALL
================================================================
1. Every file EXCEPT rdmhelper.lua goes to:
     Ashita\addons\sync\            (main only)

2. rdmhelper.lua goes to EVERY box, main included:
     Ashita\addons\rdmhelper\rdmhelper.lua

3. Load:
     /addon load sync          (main only)
     /addon load rdmhelper     (every box)
     /addon load multisend     (every box)

When rdmhelper updates later, redeploy it to every box. A "WIRE
MISMATCH" line per box after an update is the version check working
-- it clears as each box reloads rdmhelper.

wire_def.lua ships in BOTH folders and the two copies must stay
byte-identical. Always deploy them as a pair.


FIRST SESSION
================================================================
1. Open the HUD on your main.

2. Crew window: add your alts (chevron -> +/- slot buttons), then
   /addon reload sync so the roster applies.

3. Assign roles in their panels:
     healers      /sync healer
     RDM box      /sync rdm <box>
     BRD/COR/GEO  their own panels
   Skip any you don't run.

4. /sync selftest
     PASS = fine
     WARN = optional/empty (no RDM assigned etc.) -- usually fine
     FAIL = broken. Fix before continuing.

5. /sync on
   This arms the crew: engage, weaponskills, buffing, healing.
   /sync off halts everything.

That's it. Configure first, then arm.


================================================================
FULL FEATURE LIST
================================================================
Everything sync does, by subsystem. Each entry is the feature and
the command that reaches it; the panel is usually the same word.
Detail for any of these lives in readme.txt under the same heading.

--- HEALING & SUPPORT ------------------------------------------
Healers            Multiple healer slots, each with its own cure
                   tiers, thresholds and extras. Tiers and AoE are
                   derived from the box's LIVE main/sub job, so a
                   job change is picked up without reconfiguring.
                     /sync healer
Cure / Curaga      Threshold-driven single-target and AoE cures,
                   with an emergency mode that pierces the normal
                   move-settle and buff gates when someone is
                   genuinely in trouble.
WHM-first deferral A non-WHM healer briefly defers a fresh cure
                   while a live WHM healer can take it, so the
                   better cure wins instead of the faster one.
Status removal     Tiered -na. Doom and Petrify always fire; the
                   mid tier holds during a cure emergency. Works
                   cross-party. Yagrush AoE -na is honoured.
Party Regen        One-shot AoE Regen helpers. r4 pops Accession
                   then Regen IV party-wide (SCH main or /SCH 40+).
                   r5 adds Perpetuance for a duration-extended
                   Regen V -- SCH MAIN only, since both Perpetuance
                   and Regen V are main-job. Both refuse to fire
                   while the healer is mid-cast or already holding
                   an Accession reservation, so a double-tapped
                   macro cannot eat the charge and land the Regen
                   single-target.
                     /sync r4     /sync r5
Reraise            Per-healer Reraise upkeep, capability-gated:
                   WHM main or /WHM casts it directly, SCH main or
                   /SCH needs Addendum -- which is folded into the
                   existing Arts maintenance.
Healer extras      Per-healer self-buffs: Regen, Protectra /
                   Shellra (auto-tiered to what the box can
                   actually cast), Auspice, Bar-element,
                   Bar-status, Boost, Blink, Aquaveil.
                     /sync sb        (state report)
SCH Arts           Light / Dark Arts upkeep with Addendum,
                   Accession and Perpetuance, driven by command.
                     /sync arts <box|all> on|off|la|da
                     /sync la [box|all]     /sync da [box|all]
SCH 5-state engine Per-buff modes -- off / buff / perp / acc /
                   accperp -- for Protect, Shell, Regen, Phalanx,
                   Enspell, Blink, Aquaveil. Priority IS the row
                   order: Protect and Shell take the opening
                   stratagem charges, then Regen, then the rest.

--- RDM ---------------------------------------------------------
RDM buffs          Haste, Refresh, Phalanx, Enspells and the rest
                   across the crew, Composure-aware and ordered so
                   the RDM's own Haste / Refresh come first.
                     /sync rdm <box>     /sync buff
Accession blankets A RDM subbing SCH 40+ blankets Protect V /
                   Shell V and the Phalanx / Enspell line with one
                   self-cast instead of one cast per member.
RDM debuffs        Per-mob debuff queues with landed-confirmation,
                   scoped to the main's target or to every
                   crew-engaged mob.
                     /sync debuff | dpanel
Job debuffs        BLM DoTs (Burn / Frost / Rasp / Choke / Shock /
                   Drown), SCH helixes, BRD Requiem / Elegy /
                   Nocturne. All default off, one shot per mob.

--- BUFF CONTROL ------------------------------------------------
Priority chips     Per-family High / Medium / Low ordering,
                   drag-and-drop reorderable, with right-click
                   per-family disable.
                     /sync priority | prio
Action priority    Per box: when healing, -na, debuffing and damage
                   all want the same tick, this decides the order.
                     /sync prio <box> <heal|-na|debuff|damage>
Arm plan           An explicit grid of what /sync on should turn
                   on, per box. Authored once; travels with
                   profiles, so save time no longer matters.
                     /sync arm

--- HUNT & COMBAT -----------------------------------------------
Pack hunting       Boxes acquire inside a radius, respect claims,
                   and each takes a DISTINCT mob instead of piling
                   on one. Converge mode teams up on a mob the
                   crew already has, for faster kills.
                     /sync hunt      /sync radius
Whitelist          What a box IS allowed to hunt. EXACT match.
                   Empty = anything in radius.
Blacklist          What it must avoid. SUBSTRING match, so one
                   entry catches a naming family. Blacklist BEATS
                   whitelist. Global layer plus a per-box layer;
                   a per-box '-entry' negates a global one for
                   that box alone.
                     /sync wl | bl [<box|all>] [add|rm|clear]
Enemy pet sweep    One blacklist entry ( 's ) removes every BST /
                   DRG / SMN / PUP / GEO pet in the game, backed
                   by a live owner-mode pet filter with a
                   fail-open guard so a bad read cannot stall
                   the hunt.
Camp               Anchor the crew to a spot with its own radius.
                     /sync camp
Pulling            A puller draws mobs in from range, claim-gated
                   so /attack only follows a pull that took.
Defend             Per box, off by default. Answers anything that
                   starts hitting the party even with Hunt off,
                   ignoring the whitelist. Never picks fresh mobs.
                     /sync defend [on|off]
Charm sleep        A charmed crew box gets slept first so it stops
                   beating on you.
                     /sync charm [on|off]
Reactions          Event-driven responses -- a box acts the moment
                   a condition is seen, not on a timer.
                     /sync react [on|off]
Target readout     Print the name and entity index of whatever
                   the main currently has targeted -- the exact
                   string a whitelist/blacklist entry needs.
                     /sync tname

--- WEAPONSKILLS & SKILLCHAINS ----------------------------------
AutoWS             Per box, at a TP threshold, with an alternate
                   WS while Footwork is up. Remembers a setup PER
                   MAIN JOB.
                     /sync ws [box] [on|off]
AutoRA             Ranged attack on a cadence, per box.
                     /sync ra <box> [on|off]
Skillchains        An ORDERED chain: step 1 fires, step 2 follows
                   into it, and so on. SCH boxes can open with
                   Immanence. Missing scdata disables only SC --
                   it never breaks the rest of sync.
                     /sync sc [on|off]
Magic burst        Nukers burst into the crew's own skillchains,
                   flagged off the LANDED skillchain packet.
                     /sync mb [on|off]
SATA               Sneak / Trick Attack positioning with a TA
                   anchor selector and Bully detection.
Job abilities      Per-box toggles on the Combat panel, level-
                   gated and populated from that box's live job.
                     /sync ja <box|all> <ability> [on|off]
Facing calibration Per-box facing / arc calibration for positional
                   work.
                     /sync faced

--- MOVEMENT ----------------------------------------------------
Follow             Keeps a box on its target. Per-box toggle on
                   the Movement panel.
                     /sync move
Fight-range (KIR)  Closes to the mob and holds a ring, proximity-
                   gated so it nudges rather than shoves.
                     /sync kir <box|all> [on|off|<yalms>]
Standoff           Holds a chosen distance from the enemy, for
                   casters and ranged boxes that should not be in
                   the pile.
                     /sync standoff <box|all> [on|off|<yalms>]
Modes              Rooted / Stationary / Move / Hunt cycling, plus
                   return-after-fight modes.
                     /sync move | movement
Cast-move lock     Per box: a follow step never interrupts a cast
                   that is already going out.

--- BARD / CORSAIR / GEOMANCER ----------------------------------
Bard               Up to 3 bards (songs are party-scoped). Five
                   slots, dummies, instrument modes, JA toggles
                   (Nightingale, Troubadour, Soul Voice, Clarion
                   Call, Marcato, Pianissimo), Pianissimo
                   per-member overrides, and named sets.
                   Does NOT re-sing on a clock: it reads what is
                   actually up and its remaining time, then
                   re-sings the set inside one Nightingale /
                   Troubadour window before the shortest song
                   drops. A member who loses songs is patched
                   alone with Pianissimo -- guests included.
                     /sync brd | sing | songs      panel
                     /sync brd on|off              start / stop
                     /sync brd1..brd3              pick the box
                     /sync brd <name> | none       assign / clear
                     /sync patch <member> [brd]    piano their set now
                     /sync resing [brd]            full re-sing now
Corsair            Up to 6 Corsairs, two rolls each. Snake Eye,
                   Fold, Crooked Cards and Random Deal, with
                   optional engaged-only mode, gambling mode,
                   range checking, a refresh cadence and party
                   announcements. Optional Dia+ Light Shot: the COR
                   fires Light Shot ~2s AFTER a Dia of any tier
                   LANDS on a crew-engaged mob, one shot per Dia
                   application per mob -- Light Shot enhances a Dia
                   that is already up, so it follows the cast
                   rather than leading it. Off by default.
                     /sync roll [start|stop]      /sync cor1..cor6
                     /sync cor <n> snake|fold|crooked|rdeal|
                       engaged|gamble|announce|range on|off
Geomancer          Up to 3 GEOs. Indi / Geo slot scheduling with
                   Full Circle prepend, a Dematerialize guard,
                   Entrust as a two-stage combo, and a manual
                   Entrust release.
                     /sync geo [start|stop]
                     /sync geo1..geo3      /sync ent | entrust
                     /sync geo gtarg <name>    bubble target
                     /sync geo entarg <name>   Entrust target

--- UTILITY -----------------------------------------------------
Absorb-TP          Per-box auto-fire countdown plus the last
                   CONFIRMED absorb -- amount, age, target, and
                   how many WS the crew has landed on that target
                   since. Records clear when the target dies.
                     /sync abs [box] [target <n>|delay <s>|fire]
                     /sync apanel
Consumables        Auto-Echo clears Silence with an Echo Drop.
                   Auto-Food re-applies food. Auto-Soda keeps
                   Regain up with a Frontier Soda -- and a box also
                   on the Adloquium roster waits ~25s first, so the
                   free cast wins. All per box, all off by default,
                   all SESSION-armed: a zone change disarms them.
                   Each gives up after 2 failures and names the box.
                   Echo and Food are Combat-panel checkboxes.
                     /sync soda [box|all] [on|off]
Kite openers       A one-shot opener for the fights that
                   still want one: Saboteur -> Gravity II ->
                   Dia III -> Frazzle III -> Distract III, with a
                   saved profile menu.
                     /sync kite     regular (Saboteur + Gravity II)
                     /sync skite    Stymie
One-shot combos    Manual AoE combos you fire yourself on the main
                   healer or RDM box: Dark Arts -> Manifestation ->
                   the spell. The box is reserved for the whole
                   window so a routine cure cannot eat the
                   stratagem charge mid-combo. They land on the
                   issuing box's current target -- relayed to the
                   caster as [t] through Multisend, so that box's
                   own cursor is irrelevant.
                     /sync msleep     Sleep
                     /sync msleep2    Sleep II
                     /sync mbreak     Break
Cast locks         No tuning. A box is released by its own 0x028
                   completion packet, so Fast Cast gear needs no
                   config. The lock is only a ceiling (base cast
                   + 1.5s) for boxes out of render range.
                   Fast Cast tuning is retired.
Humanize           Anti-metronome jitter on action timings.
                     /sync humanize [on|off]
                     /sync humanize <lo_ms> <hi_ms>
Profiles           Save, load and delete whole configs, including
                   the arm plan.
                     /sync profile save|load|del|list|none <name>
Profile sharing    Export replaces every box with a ROLE TOKEN and
                   strips character names, then Ctrl-Fs the
                   finished text for live names and ABORTS on a
                   hit. Import is sandboxed -- the file is treated
                   as data, never as code.
                     /sync profile export <name>
                     /sync profile import <file> [as <name>] [preview]
                     /sync profile shared
Presets            A named list of flag rows -- much smaller than
                   a profile. Save captures the live flags of every
                   in-zone box as explicit on/off, targeted by crew
                   SLOT so it survives a roster change.
                     /sync <preset name>           run it
                     /sync preset                  list
                     /sync preset save <name>      capture now
                     /sync preset show <name>      read it back
                     /sync preset add <name> <flag> <target> [on|off]
                     /sync preset rm <name> <row>
                     /sync preset del <name>
                     /sync preset reset            clear them all

--- LIMITS ------------------------------------------------------
Crew               18 boxes (a full alliance). Edit in the Crew
                   window, then reload sync to apply. Party
                   membership is read LIVE -- you do not configure
                   it.
Subsystem caps     BRD 3 (one per party -- songs are party-scoped),
                   GEO 3, COR 6.

--- HUD ---------------------------------------------------------
Main HUD           Crew rows with per-box arm cells (buff / heal /
                   debuff / engage / WS) and live status.
                     /sync ui | panel
Buffer glance      One always-on row per assigned BRD / COR / GEO /
                   SCH box, plus Absorb-TP. Fixed size, no
                   scrollbars; each row's chevron opens that
                   subsystem's full panel.
                     /sync buffer | menu | glance
                     /sync buffer width|height <px|auto>
Panels             Combat, Movement, Hunt, Buffs, Debuffs,
                   Healer / Advanced, Crew, Songs, Corsair,
                   Geomancer, Absorb-TP, Priority, Arm plan.
                     /sync combat | move | hunt | buff | dpanel
                     /sync healer | adv | crew | apanel
Minimize/expand    Panels collapse to a glance row from their own
                   header button; COR and GEO also take it as a
                   sub-command.
                     /sync cor min|expand
                     /sync geo min|expand


================================================================
READING THE HUD
================================================================
"! silent" (red)   That box has rdmhelper or Multisend missing.
                   Fix on THAT box, not the main.

"HALTED"           sync hit an error and parked itself. The
                   traceback prints once. Fix the cause, then
                   /sync on resumes -- no reload needed.


IF SOMETHING DOESN'T WORK
================================================================
Box does nothing at all
    /sync selftest. Nine times out of ten it's rdmhelper or
    Multisend missing on that box.

Box won't cast / "Unable to cast" loop
    Nothing to tune -- cast locks are packet-released (0x028
    completion). Check the box is inside the main's render range:
    with no packet the lock falls back to a base-cast + 1.5s
    ceiling. See readme COMMON PROBLEMS for the rest.

Box isn't weaponskilling
    AutoWS boots OFF on every reload, on purpose. Arm it on the
    crew row's W cell.

An Accession buff takes forever to go up
    Every Accession costs a stratagem charge, and charges come
    from the SUB job's level -- 1 below 30, 2 at 30, 3 at 50 --
    with the recast being 240s split across them (240 / 120 / 80).

    Sub level is half the main job's, PLUS Master Levels: the sub
    cap is 49 + ML/5, so ML5 puts /SCH at 50 and unlocks the third
    charge, and ML50 reaches 59. Four and five charges are MAIN
    SCH only. A pre-master RDM/SCH sits at 2 charges; a RDM/SCH at
    main 59 (sub 29) has a single four-minute charge.

    Protect and Shell are ordered ahead of Phalanx / Enspell for
    exactly that reason. Check the charge count before assuming a
    fault.

A buff row never fires
    /sync sch5dbg names the gate that stopped it -- no caster,
    master Buff cell off, action lock, backoff, buff already
    present, or not readable.


DIAGNOSTICS
================================================================
/sync selftest      PASS / WARN / FAIL health check (aka diag)
/sync roles         who is assigned to what
/sync huntdbg       hunt candidate / claim decisions
/sync repdbg        rdmhelper report traffic
/sync sch5dbg       why an sch5 buff row is or isn't firing
/sync cmdlog        outgoing command relay log
/sync perf          frame / logic / UI timing overlay
/sync gc            list or remove orphaned config entries
/sync ver           version + wire version


WHERE TO GO NEXT (readme.txt)
================================================================
Once the basics run, readme.txt covers, in this order of likely
need:
    HUNT & CAMP        pack hunting, whitelists/blacklists, camps
    RDM BUFFS/DEBUFFS  who gets what and why a buff isn't firing
    BARD / COR / GEO   songs, rolls, bubbles
    SKILLCHAINS        ordered SC chains + magic bursts
    PROFILES           save/load/share whole configs
    KITE OPENERS       the four fights that want one
    DIAGNOSTICS        selftest, roles, repdbg, huntdbg

One tip worth stealing early: /sync bl add 's  blacklists every
enemy pet in the game with one entry (pets are named "<Master>'s
<Pet>" and die with their master -- fighting them is wasted time).
Details in the ENEMY PETS section of readme.txt.
